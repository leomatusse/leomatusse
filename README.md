## 🚀 Featured Projects | Projetos em Destaque

### 💧 Blue Water

**Prepaid Water Payment System | Sistema de Pagamento Pré-Pago de Água**

A Java-based application designed to manage prepaid water consumption, customer accounts, water meters, recharges, and payments.

Uma aplicação desenvolvida em Java para gerir o consumo pré-pago de água, clientes, contadores, recargas e pagamentos.

**Technologies:**

`Java` `JavaFX` `MySQL` `Hibernate` `Maven`

**Main Features | Funcionalidades:**

- Customer and water meter management.
- Prepaid water credit and recharge management.
- Water consumption simulation.
- Payment management.
- MVC architecture and Observer design pattern.

[🔗 View Repository | Ver Repositório](https://github.com/leomatusse/SPA)

### 🚔 SIGO

**Sistema de Gestão de Ocorrências | Police Occurrence Management System**

A Java-based system designed to manage police occurrences, records, involved individuals, detentions, and legal proceedings.

Um sistema desenvolvido em Java para gerir ocorrências policiais, autos de ocorrência, intervenientes, detenções e processos encaminhados ao Ministério Público.

**Technologies:**

`Java` `Object-Oriented Programming` `MVC`

**Main Features | Funcionalidades:**

- Police occurrence registration and management.
- Management of victims, witnesses, and suspects.
- Police occurrence reports (BO).
- Detention management.
- Legal proceeding management.
- Integration of decision workflows involving the Public Prosecutor's Office.

[🔗 View Repository | Ver Repositório](https://github.com/leomatusse/SIGO)
